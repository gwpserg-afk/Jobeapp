# Jobé — Flyer prompts for GPT (image) / Gemini (Nano-Banana / Imagen)

Goal: a **custom, big, clean flyer with real imagery** that explains what Jobé is.
Paste into BOTH GPT and Gemini, compare, pick the best. Brand: green **#16A34A**, off-white **#FBFAF7**,
bold sans-serif (NO serif/script), Dakar/Senegal entrepreneurial energy, honest pre-launch.

---

## ⭐ MAIN PROMPT — vertical flyer with photo (4:5, 1080×1350)
```
Design a premium, modern Instagram flyer, 1080x1350 (4:5 vertical), for "Jobé" — a mobile social
network for entrepreneurs in Senegal (pre-launch).

Layout, top to bottom:
- Top-left wordmark "Jobé" in bold sans-serif, the letter "é" in green #16A34A.
- Small uppercase eyebrow in green: "RÉSEAU SOCIAL · DAKAR".
- Big bold headline (sans-serif, tight, 2–3 lines): "Le réseau social des entrepreneurs du Sénégal."
  with the word "Sénégal" colored green #16A34A.
- A warm, authentic PHOTO: a young Senegalese entrepreneur in Dakar smiling at their phone (or a small
  group of African professionals networking), natural light, real and aspirational — NOT cheesy stock.
  Integrate it cleanly into the layout (e.g. right side or lower half), with soft rounded corners.
- 3 short benefit lines with small green check/dot icons:
  "Connecte-toi aux bonnes personnes" · "Rejoins des groupes & communautés" · "Fais grandir ton business".
- Bottom: a green pill button "Rejoins la liste → jobeapp.com" and small "@jobeapp · Bientôt sur iOS & Android".

Style: off-white background #FBFAF7, lots of whitespace, clean grid, flat modern startup aesthetic,
high contrast, crisp typography, subtle soft shadows. Brand green #16A34A as the only accent color.
No clutter, no gradients overload, no fake logos, no lorem ipsum. Professional, credible, optimistic.
```

---

## GPT vs Gemini notes
- **GPT (image)**: great at following layout + exact text. If text comes out slightly garbled, ask it to
  "render all text crisply and exactly as written, sans-serif."
- **Gemini (Nano-Banana / Imagen)**: strong photorealism for the entrepreneur photo; may need a second pass
  "keep the composition, fix the text to read exactly: …".
- Generate 1080×1350 (feed) first; then ask for a 1080×1920 (story) and 1080×1080 (square) crop of the winner.
- Avoid putting tiny text in the image (gets blurry); keep the URL + CTA large.
